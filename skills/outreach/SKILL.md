# Outreach Decision

Input: qualified prospect dossier, contact, product Knowledge and prior relationship state.

Output: action, channel, subject, message, evidence, risk flags and confidence.

This skill may create a draft, but never sends. Existing inquiry, suppression, missing contact, unverified email and unsupported product claims must block the approval path.
